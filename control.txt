^Joystick...^ Move and shoot
^Btn 2......^ Strafe
^1234.......^ Change weapon
^Shift-S....^ Quick save
^Shift-L....^ Quick load

If you have an ST mouse in port 2
and you have enabled it in the
options, you can turn and shoot with
it. The joystick then strafes.

Walk into doors to open them.
Walk over items to pick them up.
Walk into switches to activate them.
Some switches must be shot.
